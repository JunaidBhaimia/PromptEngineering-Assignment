Financial Aid

SCHOLARSHIPS & FINANCIAL AID

We believe financial constraints should never stand in the way of quality education. Explore our scholarship options.

Merit Scholarship

Up to 100% Tuition Waiver

Awarded to students who score in the top 1% of their qualifying examinations or achieve exceptional scores on the JBU Entrance Test.

Sports Excellence Award

50%–75% Tuition Waiver

For outstanding athletes who have represented their state or country in national/international competitions.

Need-Based Financial Aid

Varies Based on Circumstance

Support for students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds, determined through documented income verification.

Women in STEM Grant

Up to 50% Tuition Waiver

Encouraging female participation in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics programs.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

For most undergraduate programs, candidates must have completed higher secondary education (10+2) or an equivalent qualification with a minimum aggregate of 60% in relevant subjects. Some programs may require specific subject prerequisites (e.g., Mathematics and Physics for engineering).
Yes, JBU conducts its own Entrance Test for most programs. Additionally, we accept scores from recognized national-level exams. Shortlisted candidates are also called for a personal interview or group discussion round.
Yes, you can apply for up to three program choices in a single application. We recommend choosing programs that align with your academic background and career interests. Your preferences will be considered during the selection process.
Yes. International students are eligible for the Merit Scholarship and Need-Based Financial Aid. Additionally, JBU has exchange partnership agreements that may reduce tuition fees for students from partner universities abroad. Contact our International Office for details.
Refund policies are structured on a sliding scale. If you withdraw within the first week of classes, 100% of fees are refundable. Between weeks two and four, 75% is refunded. After four weeks, no refunds are processed. Please refer to the full refund policy document provided during registration.
Hostel accommodation is available on a first-come, first-served basis. While we aim to house all out-of-town students, we strongly recommend securing your hostel application early by selecting the housing option during the application process. Separate accommodations are available for male and female students.
