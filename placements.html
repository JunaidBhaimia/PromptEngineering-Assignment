THE JBU PLACEMENT CELL

The JBU Placement & Training Cell is dedicated to bridging the gap between academia and industry. Working closely with students, faculty, and corporate partners, we ensure every graduate is industry-ready.

Our team organizes campus drives, pre-placement workshops, aptitude training, personality development sessions, and mock interviews — creating a pipeline that connects talent with opportunity.

With relationships spanning technology, finance, consulting, healthcare, law, and more, the placement cell opens doors across every sector.

How It Works

THE RECRUITMENT PIPELINE

01

Pre-Placement Training

Students undergo structured training covering aptitude, technical skills, and interview preparation.

02

Online Assessment

Companies conduct online tests evaluating logical reasoning, coding ability, and domain knowledge.

03

Technical & HR Rounds

Shortlisted candidates face technical interviews followed by HR rounds assessing cultural fit.

04

Offer Letter & Onboarding

Selected students receive offer letters and are guided through the onboarding process.
