Our Approach

RESEARCH WITH PURPOSE

At JBU, research drives everything we do. Our faculty and students work across disciplines to tackle the world's most pressing challenges — from climate change and sustainable energy to artificial intelligence and public health. We believe that discovery begins with curiosity and ends with impact.

With dedicated research centers, state-of-the-art innovation labs, and strong industry partnerships, JBU provides an environment where bold ideas become reality. Whether you're a doctoral candidate or an undergraduate exploring your first research project, there are pathways here for every stage of your academic journey.

Get Involved

RESEARCH OPPORTUNITIES

Undergraduate Research Fellowship

Work alongside faculty mentors on active research projects during your undergraduate studies. Ideal for 3rd and 4th year students seeking hands-on experience.

Summer Research Internship

A ten-week intensive program where students contribute to ongoing research with a stipend and travel support for presenting findings at conferences.

Graduate Research Assistantships

M.Tech and PhD candidates receive funding support while working as research assistants on department-funded projects with full lab access.

Seed Grant Program

Faculty and doctoral researchers can apply for seed funding (up to INR 10 lakhs) to launch new exploratory research proposals in emerging areas.
